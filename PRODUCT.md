# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and lightweight motion. The user approved this greenfield stack and requested reusable components, strong performance, accessibility, SEO and maintainability. The project is published at `https://jumpperformace.com.br`.

## Users

Potential clients evaluating a marketing partner for their brand or company. They need to understand Jump Performance's positioning, compare its marketing packages, inspect the shape of its work and start a WhatsApp conversation from desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

The site is the institutional and commercial portfolio for Jump Performance. It must present the agency, packages, selected projects, methodology and data-led approach in one coherent page, then make contact simple through WhatsApp and social channels.

## Positioning

Jump connects strategy, creative execution and analysis in one continuous marketing cycle: information becomes direction, work enters the market, signals return as data and the next decision improves.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive through referrals, campaigns, social profiles or direct links, scan the offer, explore portfolio placeholders through accessible case-study overlays, compare packages and continue the conversation on WhatsApp. The site is a one-page commercial narrative, not a client portal or publishing platform.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One page with anchored navigation for Início, Pacotes, Projetos, Método and Contato.
- Responsive header and mobile navigation.
- Reusable package and project data models.
- Accessible fullscreen case-study dialog with keyboard focus, Escape close and background scroll lock.
- Centralized WhatsApp and social configuration with a prefilled message.
- No administration, login, ecommerce, blog, form processing, chatbot or external integration.
- No prices, testimonials, customer names, campaign metrics, ROI or other unsupported commercial claims.
- Motion must be lightweight and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- No deployment or domain configuration without separate authorization.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: JUMP PERFORMANCE.
- Voice: direct, confident, modern, strategic and premium; avoid generic agency clichés.
- Binding identity: “Data Signal” — technological, retro-futurist, editorial, dark, precise and experimental without becoming cyberpunk, gamer, crypto or generic AI.
- Binding palette: Carbon Black `#080010`, Graphite `#1A1F26`, Off White `#F5F7FA`, Electric Lime `#C8FF3D`, Electric Blue `#2D58FF`.
- Visual vocabulary: technical grids, fine lines, coordinates, signal paths, data points, restrained interface labels, negative space and measured typography.
- The official supplied logo is `public/brand/jump-performance-logo.jpeg`. Preserve its geometry, lettering and color relationships; use non-destructive presentation crops rather than redrawing it.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied Portuguese brief contains approved positioning copy, package descriptions, method steps and CTA language. The official logo is available as a WhatsApp-compressed JPEG; no vector master has been supplied. There are no real client names, case imagery, case results, verified contact details or social URLs in the repository. Those absences must remain clearly editable placeholders and must never be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Show the operating system behind the marketing, not a generic list of agency services.
2. Let project work and process carry authority; never substitute invented proof.
3. Make every contact path obvious, reusable and easy to replace with verified details.
4. Use movement to clarify hierarchy and the feedback loop, not to decorate every element.
5. Preserve fast, readable and complete use across desktop, tablet, mobile and reduced-motion settings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must provide semantic landmarks and headings, sufficient contrast, visible keyboard focus, descriptive controls, touch-friendly targets, accessible dialog behavior and a fully functional static presentation for people who prefer reduced motion.
